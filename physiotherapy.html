PhysiotherapyDr. Michael Nabil Clinic

Physiotherapy is a live clinical website made for a real client — Dr. Michael Nabil, a physiotherapy and clinical nutrition specialist based in Dokki. The goal was to bring his two specialties, physical rehabilitation and therapeutic nutrition, into one clear space, so a visitor can understand his approach, see how a treatment plan actually comes together, and reach out for a consultation without any friction.

Two specialties, one clear plan

Physical rehabilitation and clinical nutrition are presented as one connected approach rather than two separate services, so a visitor understands how a real treatment plan for orthopedic, neurological, scoliosis, or sports injury cases comes together with nutrition support.

Physiotherapy & Nutrition
[02]

A consultation request that's easy to finish

The intake form only asks for what's actually needed — name and phone, with email optional — and sits alongside direct WhatsApp and phone links, so a patient can reach the clinic in whichever way is easiest for them.

Motion that supports the content

Framer Motion adds gentle transitions between sections and small state changes throughout the page, built on top of Next.js for fast loading and Tailwind CSS for a layout that holds up cleanly on every screen size.
