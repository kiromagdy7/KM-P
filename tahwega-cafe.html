Tahwega CafeCoffee & Juice Experience

Tahwega Cafe is a live website made for a real local business—not a concept page. The goal was to give the café the same feeling people get when they walk in: warm, straightforward, and easy to enjoy. Visitors can move naturally from the opening story to the menu, get a feel for the place through customer quotes, then find the location and contact details without having to hunt for them.
